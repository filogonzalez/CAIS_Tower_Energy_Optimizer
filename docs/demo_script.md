# Five-to-seven-minute demo

1. Open the NOC cockpit and point out the **SYNTHETIC DATA** banner, frozen as-of timestamp, KPI header, and Puerto Rico/Islas map.
2. Filter to a region and open a high-priority site. Show energy source mix, HVAC/temperature behavior, component risk, and recent work orders.
3. Open Benchmarking and compare energy/GB against the `(region, site_type)` cohort. Explain that the 10–15% reduction is a scenario hypothesis, not a measured guarantee.
4. Ask the maintenance agent in English and Spanish. Expand tool/evidence traces and a similar resolved incident. Emphasize that retrieved notes are untrusted evidence.
5. Create a proposal. Show it remains `proposed`, then approve or reject as an authorized human and show the atomic audit entry.
6. Open Outage Resilience. Change outage and fuel-delivery assumptions; demonstrate a battery-only site, a late-fuel case, and synthetic population overlap assumptions.
7. Ask one of the seven governed Genie questions. Close by showing serverless bundle jobs, disabled schedules, and the Databricks-only acceptance ledger.
